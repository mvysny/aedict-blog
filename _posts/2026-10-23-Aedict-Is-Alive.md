It has been quiet here for a few years, so let me say it clearly: Aedict is alive and being worked on.
The Android app has just received a round of upgrades and fixes, and Aedict Online and the
[aedict.eu](http://www.aedict.eu/) web page are next.

# Aedict 3.52.0

The biggest news first: **Aedict can be installed from Google Play on Android 15 and newer again.**
Google Play hides apps that don't target a recent enough Android version, so if you searched
for Aedict on a new phone recently and couldn't find it, please try again.
[Aedict on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sk.baka.aedict3).

What else is new in 3.52.0:

- Support for Android 16
- The predictive back gesture
- The Settings and Welcome screens no longer draw under the status bar
- The word of the day changes daily again; it was stuck since Android 8
- Dictionary download works on Android 7.0 again
- Pending in-app purchases are handled correctly
- Better TalkBack support for icon buttons

Aedict now requires Android 7.0 or newer. Only a handful of users were still on Android 5 and 6;
if you're one of them, your current Aedict keeps working, it just won't receive updates.

The dictionaries keep being updated every month, as before.

# Aedict Online and aedict.eu

[Aedict Online](https://aedict-online.eu) hasn't been updated since 2020 and runs on a pretty old
technology stack. It's going to be modernized: rewritten on top of current libraries and moved
to a new server. The [aedict.eu](http://www.aedict.eu/) web page will get a refresh as part of the move.

This takes a while, and there's no date yet. What matters to you, though, stays the same:

- **Your Aedict Online account carries over**, and with it the commercial dictionaries such as
  [Daijirin](../Daijirin/) that you've activated.
- **Synchronization keeps working** throughout; your notepad, SRS and tags stay safe.
- **Dictionary downloads keep working**, and so does the "Aedict Online" search from within the app.

# Tell me which features you use

Aedict Online has accumulated quite a few features over the years, and I'd like to only port
those which people actually use. Please head over to
[Which Aedict Online features do you use?](https://github.com/mvysny/aedict/issues/1001)
and give a 👍 to the features you use, or leave a comment if something's missing.
No GitHub account? Just e-mail me at `martin@vysny.me`.

If you find a bug in Aedict 3.52.0, please report it at the
[Aedict bug tracker](https://github.com/mvysny/aedict/issues).

Thank you for sticking with Aedict all these years!
